The Pre-History of AI

One paragraph. Three sentences. Five days of reading, correcting, defining, and writing by hand.

This is the first daily writing artifact in your CALP notebook. The paragraph runs across five days, and all work is handwritten. On Day 5, you submit the finished paragraph and reflection to your teacher — it will be graded for content accuracy, vocabulary, and mechanics, all at once.

DAYS: 01 02 03 04 05

The Passage

Contains 10 errors — find and correct them across Days 1–3

the morning sun crept threw the gothic windows of southern mi cocina university prep Academy, casting long, austere shadows across the oak desks where a motley assembly of sixth through twelfth graders sat in tenative silence. a venerable institution known for producing standard-bearers of industry the academy have recently undergone a structural paradigm shift, manifested in a single experimental elective listed on the syllabus as "Advanced Linguistic and Artificial Intelligence Systems," though the student body universally, and somewhat derisively, referred to it as the wierd Brain Rot class. Those who signed up for the coarse had no clue that the robots in disguise were embedded in a hidden layer of a neural network all this time — running silently in the background, learning their handwriting, there hesitations, their questions, one algorithm at a time, using machine learning to predict what they would type next.

Day by Day

Day 01 · Read + Correct

Sentence 1 — Read Aloud, Mark Errors

Read Sentence 1 aloud to a partner. Then rewrite it in your CALP notebook, correcting all four errors.

the morning sun crept threw the gothic windows of southern mi cocina university prep Academy, casting long, austere shadows across the oak desks where a motley assembly of sixth through twelfth graders sat in tenative silence.

Look for

  • A missing capital letter at the start of the sentence
  • A homophone confusion (a word that sounds right but isn't)
  • A proper noun that is not fully capitalized
  • A spelling error

  • Submit your CALP notebook to your teacher

Vocabulary Reference

SAT Vocabulary

austere Severely simple; unadorned. Describing something plain and disciplined in its style or manner.
venerable Accorded great respect; ancient and dignified because of age, tradition, or accomplishment.
paradigm A typical example or pattern; a fundamental model or worldview that shapes how something is understood.
derisively Mockingly; contemptuously. Speaking or acting in a way that shows one considers something ridiculous.

Industry Vocabulary

Artificial Intelligence The field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence — understanding language, recognizing images, making decisions.
hidden layer The interior layer or layers of a neural network, sitting between input and output, where the network transforms data and learns patterns.
neural network A computing system modeled loosely on the human brain, made of interconnected nodes ("neurons") that learn patterns from data.
algorithm A precise, ordered set of computational rules or steps for solving a problem or completing a task.
machine learning A subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data to improve their performance, without being explicitly programmed for every scenario.

How This Is Scored

4 pts
Content accuracy Industry vocabulary defined correctly in Day 4 work. Concepts understood, not just memorized. Original sentences on Day 4 use the terms correctly in context.
3 pts
Vocabulary SAT vocabulary words defined correctly and used naturally. Awareness of academic register in the Day 4 sentences. Vocabulary marked clearly on Day 5.
3 pts
Mechanics All 10 errors corrected across Days 1–3 (capitalization, punctuation, subject-verb agreement, spelling, homophones). Final rewrite on Day 5 is legible, complete, and clean.
