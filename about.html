What This Course Is

This is an 18–30 week curriculum on large language models and generative AI, built for secondary students in grades 6–12. It teaches not only how to use AI tools, but how those tools actually work, where they came from, and how to think critically about them.

Instructors decide which of the six modules to combine to fit the allotted instructional time that aligns with their specific campus pacing calendar — whether that is an 18-week semester, a standard 22–24 week schedule, or a full 30-week track.

Who This Course Is For

Secondary students in grades 6–12 (middle and high school). The curriculum is built for diverse learners, including students with dyslexia and other reading-based learning differences.
